The three, again

Same three as last essay. You already know these.

Logos

LOW-goss · the logic one

MEANS
Facts, numbers, experts. Proof someone could check.
YOURS
"Statistics Canada found that 91% of 15 to 24 year olds used social networking sites."

Pathos

PAY-thoss · the feelings one

MEANS
Makes the reader feel something.
YOURS
"You and your friend open the same app and see two different worlds."

Ethos

EE-thoss · the trust one

MEANS
Why believe you? Name real sources.
YOURS
"The Competition Bureau of Canada says paid posts have to be disclosed."

The teacher asks you to name one of these in the introduction. It's the last step of paragraph 1, so you don't have to remember it.
